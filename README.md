## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and inventory data for Wanjiku, a fresh-produce stall owner at Gikomba Market. The objective is to understand her business performance by calculating total and average sales, comparing weekly performance, classifying daily sales, analysing weekday and weekend trends, identifying products requiring restocking and calculating gross profit margins. The analysis uses Python fundamentals, including functions, lists, dictionaries, loops, conditional statements and formatted output, to generate practical recommendations that can help Wanjiku improve stock management and business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Positive sales growth:** Week 2 generated KES 24,400 compared with KES 22,050 in Week 1, representing a 10.66% increase.
2. **Higher weekend demand:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared with weekday average sales of KES 2,685, an 82.50% increase.
3. **Inventory requiring attention:** Tomatoes had 18 kg against a 25 kg reorder level, while Sukuma had 9 kg against a 15 kg reorder level. Both products require restocking.
